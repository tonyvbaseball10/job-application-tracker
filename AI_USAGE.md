# AI Usage

## Tool Used

I used ChatGPT/Codex to assist with repetitive development and debugging tasks.

## Key Prompts

- Help organize Flask CRUD routes for the four required tables.
- Check the Job Match percentage logic and suggest test cases.
- Explain MySQL connection and JSON-formatting errors.

## What I Kept

I kept useful route patterns, validation ideas, test cases, and setup notes after reviewing and testing them.

## What I Changed

I matched the suggestions to my schema, adjusted field names and validation, fixed local database settings, and removed anything that did not fit the assignment.

## What I Learned

AI speeds up repetitive work, but its output still needs to be understood, tested, and corrected before use.
